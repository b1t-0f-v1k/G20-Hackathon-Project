Renewable Energy Hits Record High in 2024

Global renewable energy capacity grew by an unprecedented 12% in the first half of 2024, marking the largest six-month increase on record. Solar and wind power led the expansion, accounting for over 80% of new capacity.

Experts attribute this growth to falling technology costs, improved energy storage solutions, and stronger government policies worldwide. The International Energy Agency now predicts renewables will surpass coal as the world's largest electricity source by 2026.

June 15, 2024
By Sarah Johnson, Energy Correspondent

Electric Vehicle Sales Surge as Prices Drop

Global electric vehicle sales reached 15 million in the first quarter of 2024, a 65% increase from the same period last year. This surge comes as battery prices have fallen to record lows, making EVs cost-competitive with traditional vehicles without subsidies.

April 10, 2024
By Emily Wong, Transportation Analyst

Green Hydrogen Breakthrough Could Revolutionize Heavy Industry

Scientists at MIT have developed a new electrolyzer that produces green hydrogen at 95% efficiency, a significant leap from the current 70-80% standard. The technology uses a novel catalyst that reduces energy requirements while doubling production capacity.

January 30, 2024
By James Wilson, Technology Editor

Insect Collapse Threatens Global Food Systems

A meta-analysis of 73 long-term studies shows global insect populations have declined 45% since 1970, with climate change now the primary driver in tropical regions. The losses are most severe among pollinators like bees and butterflies that support 75% of food crops.

October 22, 2023
By Kevin O'Brien, Agricultural Scientist

Wave and Tidal Energy Projects Gain Momentum

Several large-scale wave and tidal energy plants went online in 2024, with projects in Scotland, Canada, and South Korea leading the way. These ocean-based power sources are proving to be reliable, predictable, and capable of powering thousands of homes year-round.

August 5, 2024
By Laura Stevens, Marine Energy Reporter

New Battery Technology Doubles Storage Capacity

Researchers have unveiled a solid-state battery that can store twice as much energy as current lithium-ion cells while reducing charging time to under 15 minutes. Industry experts say this could be a game-changer for renewable integration and electric vehicles.

July 18, 2024

Africa’s Largest Solar Farm Comes Online in Namibia

Namibia has launched a 500 MW solar project, now the largest in Africa, capable of supplying clean power to over 1 million households. The project is expected to reduce carbon emissions by 1.2 million tons annually and strengthen regional energy security.

AI Optimizes Renewable Energy Grids

Artificial intelligence is being used to predict energy demand and optimize the flow of renewable power across national grids, reducing waste and cutting costs. Trials in Germany have already saved millions in operational expenses.

Major cities are adopting vertical farming to grow food locally, reducing transport emissions and water usage. Singapore’s largest vertical farm now produces enough vegetables to feed 200,000 people annually.
