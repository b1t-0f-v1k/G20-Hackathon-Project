🌍 Leaf-Line: Plastic-Free Tomorrow

A campaign by a team of 2nd-year Data Science students in South Africa — building a cleaner, healthier future for all.

Why It Matters

South Africa produces more than 2 million tonnes of plastic waste each year — much of which ends up in our rivers, oceans, and communities. This pollution harms marine life, contributes to flooding when drains are blocked, and contaminates the very food we eat through microplastics.

Plastic waste isn't just an environmental issue — it's a social and economic one too. Communities living near landfills and polluted rivers face daily health risks, while the cost of cleaning up plastic burdens municipalities already struggling with limited resources.

Leaf-Line's Vision

At Leaf-Line, we believe in sustainable innovation and responsible consumption. Our Plastic-Free Tomorrow Campaign aims to inspire South Africans to rethink consumption, reduce unnecessary plastics, and take pride in protecting our natural heritage. From the Cape's oceans to the bushveld and Drakensberg mountains, our ecosystems deserve protection.

What You Can Do

  • 💧 Carry a reusable water bottle instead of buying bottled water.
  • 🛍️ Use cloth bags for shopping — say no to single-use plastic bags.
  • 🍽️ Support restaurants and cafés that use biodegradable packaging.
  • ♻️ Separate waste at home and recycle where possible.
  • 👩‍👩‍👦 Teach children the value of reusing and reducing waste.
  • 🌱 Support South African businesses creating eco-friendly alternatives.

Why South Africa Needs This

Our beautiful country is home to rich biodiversity and breathtaking landscapes. Yet, plastic waste threatens our oceans, our wildlife, and even our tourism industry. Each plastic bag, straw, or bottle we refuse is one less item choking a turtle off Durban's coast or polluting the Vaal River.

We all share the responsibility to make South Africa a leader in sustainable living across Africa.
